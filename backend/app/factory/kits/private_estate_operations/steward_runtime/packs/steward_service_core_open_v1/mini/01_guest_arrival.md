# Principal / Guest Arrival Readiness

**Status:** `platform_curated_template` · `advisory` · `requires_estate_override` · `not_governing_policy`

## Purpose
Prepare a private estate for principal or guest arrival without surprises.

## Checklist
1. Confirm arrival window, party size, and dietary/access notes with the house manager.
2. Security brief: gate/code rotation state, CCTV health, visitor list.
3. HVAC pre-condition primary suites and common rooms to estate setpoints.
4. Linen audit: beds made, spare sets staged, turndown kit ready if overnight.
5. Welcome amenities per estate preference card (never invent personal tastes).
6. Walk primary routes (drive, lift, stairs) for defects; log work orders before arrival.
7. Escalate anomalies to the house manager **before** principal notification.

## Evidence
Photograph readiness board / checklist completion. Retain in estate Layer-2 records.
