# Daily Housekeeping Reset & Turndown

**Status:** `platform_curated_template` · `advisory` · `requires_estate_override` · `not_governing_policy`

## Daily housekeeping reset
1. Reset principal and guest suites to estate standard: beds, surfaces, floors, bathrooms.
2. Restock consumables from the approved pantry list only.
3. Inspect for defects (leaks, scuffs, appliance faults); open maintenance handoff tickets.
4. Secure valuables per estate privacy instructions — never photograph personal items.
5. Complete room/common-area inspection checklist and sign shift log.

## Turndown service
1. Confirm turndown is requested for the night (estate override may disable).
2. Soft lighting, climate adjust, linen refresh, water/amenities placement per preference card.
3. Close drapes/shutters as specified; arm perimeter only if estate SOP allows staff to do so.
4. Log completion time and any exceptions for morning handover.
