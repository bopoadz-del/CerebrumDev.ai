# Laundry, Dining, Pool/Gym/Spa & Event Close-out

**Status:** `platform_curated_template` · `advisory` · `requires_estate_override` · `not_governing_policy`

## Wardrobe and laundry
- Separate principal/guest loads; follow textile care tags and estate colour codes.
- Never send restricted garments off-site without written estate approval.
- Track items in/out; escalate lost property immediately.

## Dining and pantry
- Confirm menu against dietary restrictions; use approved suppliers only.
- Pantry FIFO; log low-stock against vendor contracts.
- Service recovery: replace immediately, document, notify house manager — not the principal first unless SOP says otherwise.

## Pool, gym and spa readiness
- Water chemistry within estate ranges; chemicals stored locked (e.g. east shed pattern).
- Equipment visual check; isolate unsafe kit and open work orders.
- Towels, temperature, and access control verified before guest use.

## Event preparation and close-out
- Run-of-show with staffing plan; privacy zones marked.
- Close-out: waste, security reset, asset return, incident notes in Layer-2 only.
