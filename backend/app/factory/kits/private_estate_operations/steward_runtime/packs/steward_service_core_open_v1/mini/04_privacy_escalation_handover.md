# Privacy, Escalation, Handover, Lost Property & Turnaround

**Status:** `platform_curated_template` · `advisory` · `requires_estate_override` · `not_governing_policy`

## Privacy, discretion and restricted information
- Need-to-know only. Do not discuss principal movements with vendors or guests.
- Restricted folders/rooms require dual control when estate policy says so.
- Never cite hospitality chatbot/intent datasets as estate policy.

## Service recovery and escalation
1. Stabilize guest/principal experience.
2. House manager review.
3. Principal notification only after manager gate (unless imminent safety risk).

## Shift handover
- Written handover: open work orders, arrivals, vendor visits, keys/codes changes.
- Verbal brief for oncoming lead; acknowledge receipt.

## Lost property
- Log item, location, time, finder; secure in designated locker; notify house manager.
- Do not dispose without estate retention rules.

## Defect reporting and maintenance handoff
- Capture asset id, severity, photos (no people/PII), and vendor escalation path.
- Housekeeping defects that block readiness escalate same-day.

## Check-out / check-in style turnaround
- Departing party: linen strip, amenity reset, minibar/pantry audit, damage walk.
- Arriving party: apply guest arrival readiness checklist; confirm estate overrides.
